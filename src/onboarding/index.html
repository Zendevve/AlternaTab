Supercharge Your Tab Switching

Welcome! alternaTab brings native, keyboard-driven Alt+Tab speed directly to Google Chrome. Let's customize your activation experience first.

⏱

Hold Mode Default

Hold Alt and tap Q to cycle. Release Alt to switch instantly. Zero mouse clicks.

👆

Toggle Mode

Press Alt+Q to open. Overlay stays open; press Enter or click to switch. press Esc to close.

Practice cycling tabs below. Focus the sandbox, hold Alt and tap Q (or use the on-screen controls) to cycle. Release keys to switch!
